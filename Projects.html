Earth Dev Release:

Two waring nations, a power-hungry warlord and a... rock, creature---thing?
Looks like there's a lot to be done to restore the world of Lapis Magnus back to its former state of peace. What impeccable timing to ------ start learning to earth-bend so you can save the world before it shatters into chaos.
Yep, sounds easy enough; let's do it!
Set a coarse down the the path of legends in this unique, action-filled adventure and master the ways of the boulder as you seek to achieve the impossible!
Earth promises to take you on a great journey of adventure, challenge and perseverance, introducing players to an intuitive concept to manipulate rocks and boulders alike.
This game is inspired by earth bending styles in the series "Avatar - The Last Airbender" with an emphasis on the power of the movements in the manipulation of rocks, boulders etc.
The full game will arrive in the coming months but we sadly don't have a release date.
