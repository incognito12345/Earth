A First Encounter

For those who don't know, Earth is an intuitive and highly unique indie game project which started out as a small demonstration of rock manipulation in Unity. Due to it's immense popularity and roaring success it grew into a full blown project.

What started as a dismissive cub, came to be a ferocious lion - leading to the construction of the project undergoing multiple years of development. Remaining to this day, constantly improving and expanding. It was not until recently when at last, hints of the full game release began to show.

Hidden in the shadows for so long, Earth came to the brink of being all but forgotten... it was at this critical moment when an ingenious software engineer and a team of modeling experts decided to combine their efforts and revive this hidden gem.
Thanks to their outstanding performance, the dream team have managed to achieve what would appear to be seemingly impossible: releasing the first samples of the game to come.

Brilliant

At this stage, you may be wondering what exactly is all the commotion about? There are so many other good and pioneering games out there, what makes this one do different? Well my friend, you have much to learn.

What makes Earth so unique are it's one-of-a-kind mechanics and state-of-the-art gameplay so loved by all and highly praised amongst the testing staff at EarthGaming LTD ©®™.

The implementation is simplicity itself but the results astounding. However, one may not properly refer to a game as brilliant without astonishing graphics and visuals which truly make the overall experience magical. This game really is a unsung wonder.

It is at this moment when you may be itching to grab a copy of this game for yourself. Rest assured, you won't have to wait long... as in the words of the lead developer,
"We hope to finalize a full working version of the game in the coming months. Unfortunately, due to a lack of funding it will certainly be a challenge to gift the world this masterpiece. We have henceforth decided to unlease the game in smaller demos to build much needed support and to secure the funds necessary to provide the full game."
